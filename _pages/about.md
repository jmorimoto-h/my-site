Hello! I am a Ph.D. candidate in Statistics at UC Berkeley, Graduate Student Fellow at the Stanford University Law School RegLab, and Analyst for the World Bank Group. My research tackles problems in law and machine learning. I am a Fulbright Scholar, and have also had previous research appointments with Harvard University and the University of Tokyo (Institute of Social Science). I am also a former Associate at Kirkland & Ellis LLP. 

I hold a J.D. from Harvard University and a B.A. in Mathematics from Case Western Reserve University. 

I have also worked with Western Ballet, Dance Connection Palo Alto, Cardinal Ballet Company, Ballet Company at Berkeley, and the Harvard Ballet Company as a performing artist.
